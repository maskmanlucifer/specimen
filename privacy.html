Privacy

Specimen is an open-source web app that runs in your browser. There is no Specimen server and no analytics. This page says what data the app uses, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

Without an account

Your boards, media files, plugins and settings are stored in your browser (IndexedDB) on your device. They are not sent anywhere. You can export them as a .zip, and you can delete them in Settings → Danger zone → Clear this browser.

With Google sign-in

When you choose Google Drive, you sign in with Google through Supabase, a hosted authentication service. The app then uses these Google data:

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Your name, email address and profile pictureTo show who is signed in and to write your name on boards you publishYour Supabase account record and your browser
Access to Google Drive files that Specimen creates (drive.file)To save and open your boards in My Drive/SpecimenYour files stay in your Google Drive
A Google refresh tokenSo that you stay signed in without a new Google sign-in every hourEncrypted (AES-GCM) in a database table that only the server-side token function can read

Specimen cannot see your other Drive files, photos or documents. It does not keep a copy of your boards outside your Drive.

Published boards

When you publish a share link, the app copies the public parts of the board (no private cards, no research log) to a published folder in your Drive and makes those copies readable by anyone with the link. Unpublish deletes the copies. A static site that you download is a file on your computer; you decide where to put it.

Google API data

Specimen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Remove your data

  • Settings → Danger zone → Disconnect Google revokes the Google token and deletes the stored refresh token.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your boards in Google Drive stay until you delete the Specimen folder.

Your own Supabase project

If you connect your own Supabase project in Settings, your sign-in and token go to that project, which you control. This policy then covers only the app code.
